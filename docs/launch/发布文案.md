### 正文

这段时间我一直在想：我们越来越频繁地和 AI 一起工作，但回头看时，往往只剩下一种很模糊的感觉——好像很忙，却说不清哪天真正集中、时间主要花在哪、某个高峰到底对应什么工作。

所以我做了 AI Usage Strata。

现在它到了 v1.0.0：从第一次填写、表格或 JSON 导入、让自己的 AI 整理，到查看日期高峰、工作分类和当天证据，已经是一条完整链路。

它是一个本机优先的可视化账本：你只记录自己愿意留下的数据，页面就把时间、输入、输出、工作分类和当天证据，整理成一段可以旋转、筛选和回看的“时间地层”。

它有几个我很在意的边界：

- 默认没有任何你的数据；示例要你自己点开才会加载。
- 不注册账号、不上传、不扫描聊天记录；账本只保存在你选择的浏览器里。
- 已记录和估算会明确分开；没有依据的空白，不会被包装成数字。
- 你可以手填、导入 CSV／TSV／JSON，也可以让自己的 Codex 或 Claude 先把零散记录整理成标准账本，再由你检查导入。

我把它开源出来，希望它不只服务于“统计 AI 用了多久”，而是帮助研究者、创作者、开发者和小团队，留下更诚实的工作证据。

## LinkedIn｜English

I built and open-sourced AI Usage Strata — a local-first visual ledger for the work you choose to keep about working with AI.

It turns a small personal ledger into a readable record of time, input, output, work categories, and retained day-level evidence. There are no accounts, analytics, uploads, or chat-history scraping. Recorded values stay distinct from estimates, so uncertainty is visible rather than hidden.

You can start by entering a date and hours, importing a CSV/TSV/JSON file, or asking your own Codex or Claude workflow to structure a ledger that you review before import.

Try it: https://novakepler513.github.io/ai-usage-strata/
Source: https://github.com/NovaKepler513/ai-usage-strata

I would value feedback on import adapters, accessibility, translations, and the ways people want to inspect AI-assisted work without giving up privacy.

## GitHub Release｜v1.0.0 短说明

AI Usage Strata is now available as an open-source, local-first AI work ledger.

- Explore time, text volume, work categories, and retained evidence in one visual record.
- Start empty by default; load only a fictional example when you choose.
- Enter data manually, import CSV/TSV/JSON, or use your own AI to prepare a reviewable ledger.
- No accounts, analytics, uploads, remote storage, or automatic file/chat scanning.
- Chinese and English interfaces; MIT-licensed source.

Please use Issues for bug reports and ideas, and keep personal ledgers and identifying evidence out of public threads.
