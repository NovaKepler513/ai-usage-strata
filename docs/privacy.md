# Privacy and safe use

## What the app does

- Reads only the records you enter directly, or the JSON/CSV/TSV file you deliberately choose in the browser.
- Stores that imported copy in the current browser’s local storage.
- Lets you download that same copy, clear it back to an empty ledger, or load the separate fictional example only when you ask to see it.
- Can generate a task guide for an AI you choose to use. The guide is text in
  your browser; it does not connect to that AI or transfer any data.

## What the app does not do

- It has no account, server, analytics, tracking pixel, network request, or
  automatic sync.
- It does not scan your chat history, files, calendar, repository, or browser.
- It does not send the imported ledger anywhere.
- It does not connect to Codex, Claude, ChatGPT, or any other AI account, and
  it cannot grant an AI permission to read your computer.

## Your responsibility

Browser storage belongs to the browser profile, not to the app alone. Use a
separate browser profile on a shared computer, export encrypted backups if
your ledger is sensitive, and do not import a ledger into an untrusted webpage
that only resembles this project.

Before sharing a ledger or a screenshot, remove evidence labels and links that
could reveal clients, collaborators, private file locations, or raw chat text.

## When you use an AI to prepare a ledger

The AI provider and the permissions you give that AI are separate from this
website. A local coding AI may be able to read and write in a folder you
authorise; a cloud AI can only use material you deliberately upload or paste.
Start with one explicit folder or export, ask the AI to state its source scope,
and import the resulting ledger only after you review it. See
[AI-assisted import](ai-assisted-import.md).
